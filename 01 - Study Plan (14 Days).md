# 14-Day Study Plan

Day 1 is Tuesday 2026-11-17 and Day 14 is Monday 2026-11-30. Shift the dates if your exam is on a different day. Budget about 90 minutes to 2 hours a day.

> [!important] Daily routine (same order every day)
> 1. **Flashcards due today** (15–30 min). Do these first, every day, without exception. Spaced repetition only works if you clear the due pile daily.
> 2. **New material** (45–60 min). Read the day's notes, then answer each note's *Quick self-check* from memory.
> 3. **New cards** (15–20 min). Start the deck listed for the day.
> 4. **Practice questions** (15–30 min) on the days listed. For every miss, write down *why the right answer is right and why your answer was wrong*, as you did in the `Assessment Test` folder.

Target scores: 85% or higher on each practice set before moving on, and 90% or higher on the mock exam by Day 13.

## Exam-day technique

- Read the last sentence of the question first; it tells you what is being asked.
- Underline the qualifier: *most cost-effective*, *least operational overhead*, *fully managed*, *serverless*, *globally*, *private*, *dedicated*. The qualifier usually eliminates two options.
- For "Select TWO", the screen tells you how many to choose. Choose exactly that many.
- Flag and move on if a question takes over 90 seconds. You have about 83 seconds per question.
- Never leave a blank; there is no penalty for guessing.
